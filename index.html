B. Fort Collins & NoCo Tap Water Facts
  • Sources: Cache la Poudre River (affected by Cameron Peak wildfire ash/runoff) & Horsetooth Reservoir (seasonal algae blooms / Geosmin dirt taste).
  • Free Chlorine (~1.3 mg/L): Pumped in to kill algae and bacteria; absorbs into skin in 15 seconds and vaporizes into chemical steam in hot showers.
  • Disinfection Byproducts (TTHMs ~31 ppb / HAA5 ~22 ppb): Formed when chlorine reacts with wildfire runoff & organic matter. Legal by EPA rules, but 21x to 46x above EWG health guidelines and linked to long-term cancer risk.
  • City vs. Hard Water Zones: Fort Collins city water is naturally soft (1.5–3 GPG, 50–110 ppm TDS — no softener needed). Wellington (80549), Windsor (80550), Timnath (80547), and rural wells have severe rock hardness (12–25+ GPG) that chokes pipes and wastes $40–$60/mo in extra soap.
