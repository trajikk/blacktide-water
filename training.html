Part 1 • Know Your Territory

The Fort Collins Water Intel Report

Before you knock a single door, know the local water chemistry better than the city engineers. Fort Collins gets its water from two sources: The Cache la Poudre River and Horsetooth Reservoir.

🚨 1. The Crucial Trap: DO NOT Test Hardness or TDS in Fort Collins City Limits!

Unlike 85% of America, Fort Collins municipal water is naturally very soft (around 1.5 to 3 grains per gallon) and has very low TDS (Total Dissolved Solids are usually only 50–110 ppm).

  • The Mistake: If you pull out a TDS meter or hardness strips in Old Town or South Fort Collins, the water will pass with flying colors. The homeowner will say, "See! Our water is amazing, get out of my house."
  • The Exception: If you drive 15 minutes north to Wellington (80549), east to Severance / Timnath (80547) / Windsor (80550), or knock on rural well-water homes in Laporte or Bellvue, the water is horrific—super hard, high TDS (400–800+ ppm), and full of sulfates and iron. Save your hardness/TDS tests (and switch your presentation deck to Hard Water Mode) for those areas.

2. What IS Wrong With Fort Collins City Water? (Your Selling Ammo)

  1. Heavy Chlorine (~1.3 mg/L): To kill bacteria and algae from Horsetooth Reservoir, the city pumps heavy free chlorine into the lines.
  2. Cancer-Linked Disinfection Byproducts (TTHMs & HAA5s): When chlorine mixes with burned forest runoff and organic plant matter in the Poudre River, it creates chemical gases called Trihalomethanes (TTHMs) and Haloacetic Acids (HAA5). According to the Environmental Working Group (EWG), Fort Collins water tests 21x to 46x+ above the recommended health guideline for these carcinogenic byproducts.
  3. The "Horsetooth Dirt Taste" (Geosmin): Every late summer and fall, algae blooms in Horsetooth create compounds called Geosmin and MIB that make tap water smell and taste like lake bottom dirt.
  4. Fluoride & Dissolved Minerals at the Kitchen Tap: Fort Collins adds Fluoride to municipal water, which whole-house carbon tanks do not remove—making the Kitchen Under-Sink RO combo the complete 1-2 punch.

Part 5 • Kitchen Sink Theater

The 3-Step Fort Collins Kitchen Sink Demo

As soon as you step up to the sink, tap "📱 QR / Sink Link" in the bottom bar of your presentation deck and let the homeowner scan it with their phone so they follow along on sink.html while you run the drops!

Step 1: The "Skin Absorption" Chlorine Trick (The Ultimate Closer)

  1. Take two clear plastic cups. Fill both halfway with their cold kitchen tap water.
  2. Say: "Okay, these are both identical cups of your tap water. Can you do me a favor? Stick two fingers into this left cup and swirl them around for 15 seconds." (Have the wife or husband swirl their fingers in Cup A).
  3. Take your OTO Chlorine Drops (yellow) or DPD Powder Packets (bright pink). Put 4 drops into Cup B (untouched tap water) and 4 drops into Cup A (the cup their fingers were in).
  4. The Result: Cup B turns bright yellow/pink. Cup A stays 100% crystal clear!

THE SCRIPT: "Wait... why did this cup turn bright yellow with pool chlorine, and the one you touched stay completely clear? Where did the chlorine go?"

(Pause and let them say: "Into my fingers?!")

YOU: "It went straight into your fingers in 15 seconds. Chlorine is designed to bind to organic matter instantly. Your skin is your body’s largest organ. Even if you use a fridge filter or Brita to drink, every time you or your kids take a 10-minute hot shower, your pores open up and your body absorbs more chlorine and chemical steam than if you drank 8 glasses of pool water. Ever notice how dry and itchy your skin gets here in the winter? Everyone blames the Colorado air—it’s actually the hot chlorinated water stripping your skin."

Step 2: The EWG Zip Code Shock (For the Analytical Homeowner)

On their phone (via sink.html) or your iPad, open ewg.org/tapwater for their Fort Collins zip code (80525, 80521, 80524, 80526, 80528).

THE SCRIPT: "Look what it says under Potential Effect for HAA5 (21x limit) and TTHMs (46x limit): Cancer risk. Step 3: The Freezer Ice Cube Smell Test

Have them grab a handful of ice cubes from their freezer, drop them in a dry cup, and smell them. Freezing concentrates trapped gases and Horsetooth Geosmin odor so they can immediately smell the stale chemical scent.
